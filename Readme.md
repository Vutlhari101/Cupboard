# Cupboard 

> Keep track of what’s in your kitchen, your pantry, or your personal collections.

Ever ran out of grocery items because you swore there was another can in the back of the cupboard? **Cupboard** is a straightforward inventory manager built to give you real-time visibility into your household supplies, hobby gear, or collectables.

---

### Key Features

* **Quick Stock Logging:** Add, update, and remove items in seconds with simple quantity counters.
* **Low-Stock Alerts:** Set threshold warnings so you know what needs replacing before you run out completely.
* **Custom Categories & Locations:** Track items by room (Pantry, Fridge, Basement) or by collection type (Board Games, Vinyl, Tools). (SOON)
* **Search & Filter:** Find exactly what you have on hand without tearing open every cabinet.(SOON)

---

### Tech Stack

* **Frontend:** React
* **Backend:** Node.js
* **Database:** PostgreSQL
